Privacy policy

Written for app stores and for anyone who wants to know what stays on the phone. It covers the Grzybobranie AI app and this website.

As of 22 October 2026.

Recognition

Recognition is meant to run locally, on a model on the phone. A mushroom photo is not sent to an online recognition service. The test version does not include a calibrated model yet, so the scanner does not name a species.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account and it does not ask for an email address.
  • It has no online mushroom database that the app would query for cards.
  • It does not store photos or the journal in the cloud.
  • It does not show ads and it does not use analytics.

What stays on the device

Photos
A photo from the scanner, the gallery, or the journal stays on the phone. The app uses the camera and, on iPhone, the photo library only to open that file on the device.
Journal
Entries (note, date, photo, place) are stored locally. There is no sync between phones.
Location
A find’s place is used only on the device, when you choose to save a journal entry. The app does not track location in the background. It does not send coordinates today.

Weather and maps — planned

This version has no weather and no maps. Both are planned.

If weather is enabled, only coordinates rounded to about 1 km may be sent to a weather API. The exact position stored in the journal is not sent for that.

Maps, if they are added, will show OpenStreetMap data. That is a separate map service, not a store for photos or the journal.

This website

The site is static. It sets no cookies, it has no form, and it does not load fonts, analytics, or other scripts from elsewhere.

Contact

Privacy questions and bugs: issues on the GitHub repository. The repository does not publish a company address or a legal entity. Until it does, that issues page is the contact.

Do not eat a mushroom based only on this app. Poisoning: 112 or 999. Toxicology-centre numbers are in the app’s Help section (Pomoc).
